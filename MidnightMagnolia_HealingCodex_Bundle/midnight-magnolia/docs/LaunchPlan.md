## 30-Day Rollout Ritual
The first month centers ease: quiet build weeks paired with intentional unveilings. Each phase ends with a grounding rest day.

### Week 1 — Root the Story (Days 1–7)
- **Day 1:** Confirm brand system, lock color variables, sync Google Fonts.
- **Day 2:** Draft homepage copy (hero mantra, offerings, mood-ring footer messaging).
- **Day 3:** Assemble Business Plan, Launch Plan, Brand Guide, Website Info docs in `/docs`.
- **Day 4:** Prepare Notion Healing View dashboard import file; map Make.com data fields.
- **Day 5:** Record 3 audio affirmations and source ambient loops (royalty-free).
- **Day 6:** Soft QA of accessibility: contrast tests, screen reader flow for hero + nav.
- **Day 7:** Rest and reflection entry in Notion; capture insights for Week 2 polish.

### Week 2 — Build the Experience (Days 8–14)
- **Day 8:** Scaffold Next.js pages (index, shop, blog, services, contact) with shared layout.
- **Day 9:** Develop Recovery Widget component (affirmations + reflection form placeholders).
- **Day 10:** Configure Express `/api/reflections` route (CRUD stub with MongoDB schema).
- **Day 11:** Integrate mood-ring footer animation respecting ≤3% luminance shift.
- **Day 12:** Connect Make.com scenario for daily affirmation rotation into Notion.
- **Day 13:** Write onboarding email sequence inside Notion + export to Make.com.
- **Day 14:** Rest, review analytics instrumentation (Plausible, Stripe test keys).

### Week 3 — Share & Invite (Days 15–21)
- **Day 15:** Publish teaser blog post; schedule social media carousels.
- **Day 16:** Upload Shopify product CSV (Recovery Grove starter kit) and sync Etsy.
- **Day 17:** Host live virtual walkthrough (Zoom + Notion) to gather feedback.
- **Day 18:** Launch Magnolia Radio pilot episode; embed on site.
- **Day 19:** Finalize Wise.com payout automation for collaborators.
- **Day 20:** Conduct accessibility audit with external reviewer; log findings.
- **Day 21:** Rest day journaling; integrate feedback into backlog.

### Week 4 — Bloom & Sustain (Days 22–30)
- **Day 22:** Public site launch; enable Stripe + Wise live keys.
- **Day 23:** Open membership waitlist; deliver welcome ritual email.
- **Day 24:** Offer first Creative Sobriety Lab session; collect testimonials.
- **Day 25:** Release Printify merch capsule with mood-ring palette.
- **Day 26:** Submit KDP planner for approval; share preview on blog.
- **Day 27:** Pitch white-label dashboard to 3 recovery institutions.
- **Day 28:** Review KPIs inside Notion dashboard; adjust automations.
- **Day 29:** Tender community gratitude circle on Zoom; gather stories.
- **Day 30:** Rest deeply; archive metrics and write month-one reflection.

## Supporting Checklists
- **Tech:** Domain routing, SSL, environment variables, MongoDB Atlas connection, Make.com webhook testing.
- **Content:** Alt text audit, tone consistency, Adinkra motif usage log, legal disclaimers.
- **Community:** Moderation guidelines, trauma-informed consent language, crisis resource list.
- **Finance:** Stripe payout schedule, Wise currency rules, monthly reserve allocation.

## Success Signals
- Homepage bounce rate under 45% within first week.
- 150+ waitlist signups by Day 30.
- 90% of testers report feeling calmer after interacting with the Healing Codex.
- Minimum 3 partner collaborations initiated for licensing or affiliate sharing.
