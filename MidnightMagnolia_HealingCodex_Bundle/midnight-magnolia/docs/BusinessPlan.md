# Midnight Magnolia — Business Plan

## Executive Overview
Midnight Magnolia is a southern-gothic healing studio devoted to merging ritualized rest, creative expression, and digital autonomy. We guide recovering creatives into sustainable income streams by pairing compassionate curricula with calm technology. Every touchpoint honors the mantra: *Rest is strategy. Creation is power.*

## Mission & Vision
- **Mission:** Fuse creativity, technology, and recovery into sustainable income and gentle autonomy.
- **Vision:** A rooted network of healing artists who earn with ease, share resilient stories, and extend rest-forward tools across the South and beyond.
- **Values:** Soft power, ritual, transparency, cultural honor, accessibility, interdependence, becoming.

## Audience & Community
- **Primary:** BIPOC women and femmes in recovery or transition who crave ritual, narrative healing, and income streams they can maintain while resting.
- **Secondary:** Therapists, sober living communities, and wellness collectives seeking inclusive recovery resources.
- **Community Structure:** Healing Circles (Notion spaces), Recovery Grove forum, Patreon-lite membership, guided workshops, and asynchronous reflection companions.

## Brand Promise
We deliver grounded, poetic interfaces that translate recovery wisdom into daily practice. Automated systems lighten admin labor so members can focus on art, rest, and revenue. Every product is vetted for sensory accessibility (dyslexia-friendly text, no harsh motion, WCAG AA+ contrast).

## Offer Suite
- **Digital Products:** Recovery journals (Notion + PDF), mood-ring audio affirmations, Shopify-ready creative kits, KDP low-pressure planners, Printify merch.
- **Services:** Creative sobriety coaching, ritual design labs, launch audits, and group healing salons hosted virtually.
- **Content:** Blog essays, Magnolia Radio (podcast), guided meditations, weekly affirmation rotations via Make.com.
- **Licensing & Partnerships:** White-label healing dashboards for sober living programs, workshop licensing through community colleges, affiliate bundles with Etsy sellers and Wise.com global payout support.

## Revenue Model
- **Recurring:** Membership tiers ($18 calm tier, $42 soft-power tier, $88 rooted cohort) delivered via Stripe with community access gated through Notion.
- **Product Sales:** Shopify dark-mode storefront synced to Etsy, plus KDP royalties. Average product price $14–$48, with bundles at $72+.
- **Services:** One-on-one rituals start at $180, cohort labs at $360 per participant, corporate workshops $1,200+.
- **Affiliate & Licensing:** Wise.com transfer fees share, digital template licenses, co-created Printify drops (30% margin).
Projected first-year revenue goal: $72k, balancing 40% recurring membership, 35% product sales, 20% services, 5% licensing.

## Marketing & Storytelling
- **Core Narrative:** “Rest is a strategy. Creation is power.” Use poetic copy, archival textures, magnolia bloom, crescent moons, and Adinkra motifs.
- **Channels:** Instagram carousel rituals, TikTok soft-motion process clips (<3% luminance shift), Substack letters, Pinterest moodboards, podcast collabs.
- **Content Rhythm:** Weekly affirmation drop, bi-weekly recovery essay, monthly cohort showcase, quarterly ritual workshop launch.
- **Lead Capture:** Dark-mode landing page with Merriweather body copy and Poppins intake forms, connected to Make.com > Notion CRM > welcome automations.

## Operations & Technology
- **Frontend:** React/Next.js dark mode, Cormorant Garamond for headlines, Merriweather body, Poppins interface.
- **Backend:** Express API (Node 18+) with MongoDB Atlas student tier for reflections, Stripe + Wise integration for payments and payouts.
- **Automation:** Make.com orchestrates daily affirmations, Notion updates, email sequences. Slack channel for team handoffs.
- **Analytics:** Plausible privacy-first tracking, Notion KPI dashboard, Stripe financial reports, Makerlog for workflow visibility.
- **Accessibility Rituals:** Semantic HTML, aria labels, dyslexia-friendly line height (1.8×), max 70ch text width, no flashing animation, mood-ring footer transitions under 3% luminance change.
