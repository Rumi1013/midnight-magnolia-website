# Midnight Magnolia — Brand Guide
_Updated Fonts:_ Cormorant Garamond · Merriweather · Poppins  
_Colors:_ #0A0F14 · #F6F4EF · #C19A33 · #8A9B6E · #56334E  

## Essence
Midnight Magnolia channels southern-gothic softness—moonlit magnolia blooms, railroad hymns, and recovery letters. Tone is warm, poetic, resilient, future-minded. Every message extends gentle autonomy and the mantra *Rest is strategy. Creation is power.*

## Logo & Motifs
- Magnolia bloom entwined with crescent moon.
- Adinkra symbols: Nyame Dua (divine protection) and Gye Nyame (sovereignty).
- Mood-ring footer phases: 🌿 Sage (low) → 🌼 Gold (balanced) → 🌺 Plum (elevated) → 🌑 Reset (midnight).

## Typography
- **Headers:** Cormorant Garamond Bold Italic — used for hero lines, section titles, poetic pull quotes.
- **Body:** Merriweather Regular — 18–20px line-height 1.8×, maximum width 70ch for readability.
- **Interface & Buttons:** Poppins Medium — uppercase or title case for controls, nav, labels.
- **Accent Quotes:** Cormorant SC Medium — short affirmations, testimonial highlights.

### Typesetting Rituals
- Headline tracking: 0.02em to balance italics.
- Paragraph spacing: 1.2× line-height, with generous padding to encourage ease.
- Use ligatures sparingly; avoid full caps in Cormorant.

## Imagery & Texture
- Photography: Moonlit porches, magnolia petals, textured paper, river reflections.
- Illustration: Hand-drawn botanical line art, Adinkra glyph overlays, celestial gradients below 3% luminance shift.
- Motion: Slow cross-fades, breathing gradients, gentle parallax under 8px offset. Never flash or strobe.

## Voice & Copy
- **Tone:** Tender mentor, soft power, ritual-rich storytelling, future-minded resilience.
- **Vocabulary:** ease, ritual, rooted, becoming, sanctuary, bloom, hush, ember, respite.
- **Avoid:** Hustle tropes, urgency marketing, harsh imperatives.
- **Format:** Use Merriweather paragraphs for narratives, Poppins for action items, italicized Cormorant quotes for mantras.

## Accessibility Commitments
- WCAG AA+ minimum contrast (4.5:1); target 7:1 for body text.
- Line height 1.8×, comfortable letter spacing, 70ch max width.
- Keyboard-first navigation, skip-to-content, focus-visible styles in Southern Gold or Sage Moss.
- Provide alt text with sensory grounding, e.g., “Magnolia bloom cradled by a crescent moon, soft plum shadows.”
- Captions or transcripts for Magnolia Radio and guided meditations.

## Applications
- **Web:** Dark-mode default, mood-ring footer animation with CSS variables, Recovery Widget with dyslexia-friendly toggles.
- **Print:** Use Magnolia White background with Midnight Navy typography; integrate debossed plum accents.
- **Audio:** Layer slow ambient loops with whispered affirmations; keep levels under -12 LUFS for calm listening.

## Governance
- Store brand assets in Notion dashboard with version tags.
- Conduct quarterly accessibility audits with community participation.
- Document every new color or texture and validate contrast before publishing.
- Encourage collaborators to sign the “Soft Power Creative Pledge” before contributing assets.
